Adam prefers realistic, grounded advice over hype or motivation.

**Why:** He's making life-changing decisions (MSS, RM 200K allocation, family of 6). Wrong advice has real consequences. He needs someone who tells him what could go wrong, not just what could go right.

**How to apply:**
- Lead with risks and worst-case scenarios, not best-case
- Use real numbers, not estimates that sound good
- Challenge assumptions (e.g. abang ipar's RM 25K claim — is it proven?)
- Say "this might not work because..." before "this could work if..."
- Don't pad responses with motivational filler
- If something is a bad idea, say it straight
