## Pricing Structure

**Setup (one-time):**
- Basic: RM 2,500 — 1–3 staff, simple ops
- Standard: RM 3,500 — 4–8 staff, full features
- Pro: RM 4,500 — 8+ staff, multi-location, custom reports

**Monthly maintenance:**
- Basic: RM 200/mo
- Standard: RM 280/mo
- Pro: RM 350/mo

Includes: hosting, support (24hr response), updates.
Migration of existing data: RM 300–500 extra (one-time).

## Payment Terms
- Deposit: 50% before work starts
- Balance: 50% after go-live
- Monthly: starts month 1 after launch

## Timeline
- ~4 weeks total: 1 week design/setup, 2–3 weeks dev, 3–5 days testing, 1–2 days launch + training

## Target Clients
Salon, kedai gunting, spa, klinik kecik, kedai runcit — any SME without a management system.

## Key Selling Points vs Competitors
- vs Vagaro/Fresha: local support, BM, MYR pricing, no USD
- vs WESS: modern UI, transparent pricing, no long contracts
- Social proof: HMS Salon (Haida Muslimah Salon) LIVE, 463 resit RM78K migrated

## How to Apply
When Adam asks "draft quotation for [client type]":
1. Ask: jenis bisnes, berapa staff, ada data sedia ada?
2. Suggest pakej (Basic/Standard/Pro)
3. Fill template with client name, date, SQ number, selected pakej
4. Output clean quotation ready to share
